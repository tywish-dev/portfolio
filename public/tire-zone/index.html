Got a flat?

  • Put the spare on, or drive as little as you can. The longer a tire runs flat, the less likely it can be saved.
  • A nail or screw in the tread can usually be repaired if the hole is no wider than ¼ inch (6 mm).
  • Damage at the edge of the tread or in the sidewall can't be repaired safely. That tire has to be replaced, and a used tire in your size can do the job.

Find your tire size

It's printed on the side of every tire. Type it in and we'll explain what each part means, then send it with a price request so we can check stock before you drive over.

Can't find it? The sticker inside your driver's door lists the size your car came with.

225/65R17 98H

Showing the example size 225/65R17 98H.

  • 225

    Width: 225 mmAbout 8.9 inches across the tread.

  • 65

    Sidewall height: 65% of the widthAbout 146 mm (5.8 in) from the wheel to the tread.

  • R

    Radial constructionNearly every car tire made today is radial.

  • 17

    Fits a 17-inch wheelA new tire has to match this number.

  • 98

    Load index 98Each tire can carry up to 1,653 lb.

  • H

    Speed rating HBuilt for speeds up to 130 mph.

Replacing tires? Stick with the size on your door sticker or in your owner's manual unless a tire pro tells you otherwise.
