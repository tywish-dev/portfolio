Privacy policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

This policy explains what information Tire Zone NOLA ("we", "us") collects through this website, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • Price requests. When you send the price request form, we receive your phone number and anything else you choose to fill in: your name, tire size or vehicle, whether you want new or used tires, how many, and your note.
  • Technical logs. The company that hosts this website (Vercel) automatically records basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We don't use these logs to identify you.

We don't collect payment information, we don't offer accounts, and this website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. See our cookie policy for details.

How we use it

We use your price request only to answer it: to call you back about tire sizes, availability and prices. We don't add you to a mailing list, and we don't send marketing texts or emails.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. We share it only with the service providers that run this website and deliver form messages to us, and only so they can provide that service, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We delete price requests within 12 months after we've answered them. Our host keeps technical logs for a limited time under its own retention rules.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Call or email us using the details below and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

This website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). No website is completely secure, so please don't send sensitive details such as card numbers through the form.

Children

This website isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Other websites

Links to Google Maps, Apple Maps, Google reviews and Yelp take you to those services. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
