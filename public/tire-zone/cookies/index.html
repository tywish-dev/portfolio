Cookie policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser, often to remember you or track what you do across websites.

This website doesn't use cookies

  • No cookies of any kind are set by this website.
  • No analytics, advertising pixels or social media trackers.
  • Nothing is saved in your browser's local storage.
  • Fonts are stored on this website, so loading a page doesn't contact Google Fonts or other font services.

Because nothing is tracked, there's no cookie banner to accept or decline.

Other websites

Links to Google Maps, Apple Maps, Google reviews and Yelp take you to those services, which may set their own cookies under their own policies.

If this changes

If we ever add cookies or tracking, we'll update this page first and ask for your consent where it's required.
